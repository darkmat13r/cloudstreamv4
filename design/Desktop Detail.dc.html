From 7a and 7b, for a window. The poster beside the title, the meta line and two lines of synopsis that open with More. The action row follows ui-components.md: one white primary first (Resume, with its rule in live and the time left under it), the common secondaries outlined with glyph and label, then the watchlist toggle (Plus, then Check) and More, icon-only with tooltips. The TV-only ruled exception that makes the row's secondaries icon-only is not used: a window has the room for their words.

Episodes are on the page (desktop-design.md: "Episodes behind a selector" on TV, "On the page" here), under segmented season buttons. Each row: a 96×56 still with the watched rule, the number, the name, and what is left. Hovering shows Play, Download and Mark watched as 24 cells with tooltips: the list-row exception of rule 4, extended to the Mac (DEC-19).

Sources (7c) are a side panel that grows as links arrive, as the Mac's built source list does (BUG-19): the header counts answers and says loading until the search ends, a failed source keeps its row with its reason in words and a hollow glyph, never live. Cancel closes it.

Not drawn here: cast and More like this below the episodes (7a-ii's order, unchanged); the player (7d, 7e), which the Mac already has (desktop-design.md's player rows).
