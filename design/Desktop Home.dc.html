What the Mac keeps from TV Home, and what it changes. The hero (3c), Continue watching, the bookmarks rail with its status chips (3e, 3f), the source picker with None (3h, 3i), Reload and Random named in the toolbar (3j), offline with the rows kept (3g) and a reload that failed (3m). Rows are one line of cards each, which HomeScreenDesktop already does, with Load more in the header. The hero is a card inside the page, not full-bleed: a window is not a 10-foot screen and the sidebar holds the left edge.

Pointer and keys. A card lifts 4 under the pointer, opens on a click, and has a context menu: Open (⌘↓), Add to Bookmarks, Play (Space) where it can play. ⌘R reloads, the menu bar's Go menu moves between destinations (⌘1 to ⌘4, KMP-53).

The source picker is a side panel from the right over a dimmed page, as the Mac's other pickers are (desktop-design.md: "Source list replaces the page" on TV, a panel here). None is its first row and leaves the library's rows on the page.

Open. Continue watching's line is "S2 E4 · Woe's Hollow" here; TV 3c's "28m left" needs I18N-10's key. The details panel (3k, 3l) is not drawn: on a Mac, More info opens the title's page, which has the room. Words on these boards that have no key yet are listed in the task entry before any code uses them.

Measured in the task entry, as DS-18 did: boxes on the 4 dp step, no overflow, every glyph URL answering.
