CLOUDSTREAM 4 · SHARED COMPONENT KIT v0.1

Build these before any screen.

The audit found four primitives reimplemented about five times each in divergent XML — PosterCard alone replaces seven layouts and is used by nine screens, and twelve separately-named “new” row components across six clusters turned out to be one anatomy. Everything here is drawn at TV artboard scale; halve for dp. Focus states are shown live, and every focusable honours the 8px ring footprint and the 96px minimum target from v0.2.

01 · INTERACTION PRIMITIVES

Everything composes these five

BUTTON · h64 · min-width 240
Resume S2 E4 Resume S2 E4 Watch trailer Unavailable Watchlist
Primary is white fill, black label, and there is one, first. A common action leads with its glyph, 32 square and 16 from the label, in the label’s colour; one that no glyph conveys stays text. Icon-only is for a toggle, overflow or search: a 64 tile in a 96 target that names itself under the cell when focused; a toggle on is an ink glyph on a raised fill, off a muted glyph. There is no coloured button in this system. Disabled is 40% opacity and stays focusable so a loading screen never has zero focus owners.
ICON BUTTON · 48 VISUAL IN A 96 HIT BOX
The ring is drawn on the 48px visual, evenly, as on chips; the 96px hit box stays the target — that is what makes an icon button meet the minimum without looking heavy.

05 · SIDE PANEL

The TV answer to a bottom sheet

Bottom sheets stay the mobile idiom. On TV they are replaced by an animated side panel that slides in from the right, 560px wide, full height, with the content behind it scrimmed but not unmounted. Focus moves into the panel and is trapped there; BACK slides it out and returns focus to the exact control that opened it.
