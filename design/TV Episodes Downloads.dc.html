CLUSTER 6TV — Episodes and downloads

Five 1920x1080 Android TV artboards on the shared chassis: the episode selector as a 560 side panel over a scrimmed title detail, the episode row pinned as a component at both widths it ships at, and the three downloads surfaces — titles, one series, and the empty screen carrying the offline and storage-full banners. Everything here is drawn against the corrected contract: hairlines are inset shadows so nothing shifts a padding box, focus rings are outlines at 4px offset, the lift is 8, chip visuals are 48 and glyphs 32, and every text stack in a 96 row totals 64 so it centres at +16. The facts on screen bind to ResultEpisode, DownloadType and DownloadEpisodeCached as they exist today, or are named in the closing note as engineering prerequisites with file:line evidence.

A failure here is a hollow neutral marker, never live (DEC-17). live is playback state only, so failure reads from shape and words. Progress of every kind — watch position on 6a and 6b, transfer on 6b, 6c and 6d — draws as a 4px ink fill on a line track. A failed row carries the hollow 16px mut marker, plus the stalled mut bar when a transfer stopped at a known point; the storage-full banner carries the same marker and a 4px mut leading rule. The rail logo mark draws in ink, not --acc, and both widths on 6b state the failure the same way: the marker, the ink2 alert mark, the stalled mut bar and the words. A failure still needs its own mark, and the reason is in the engine, not in taste: a failed download has no re-entry edge at all — EpisodeDownloadInstance.isFailed is documented "Do not automatically retry in case of failure" (DownloadManager.kt:1742-1744) and there is no IsFailed → IsPending transition anywhere — so an unnoticed failure is permanent, while an unnoticed progress bar corrects itself a second later. That also settles the trailing glyph: on a failed row the error mark and the reason string are ink2, never red.

A 96-tall row has one 64-tall text stack, and that is the whole answer to "what drops at 472". Rule 5 forces the stack to total a multiple of 16 to centre in a 96 row, so the row runs Manrope 24/32 plus an 8 gap plus mono 20/24 — 64, centring at +16 — at both widths. A 28/40 title would make the stack 72 and land the text at +12, off the step, which is why 6b's 1664 specimens keep the same type as the 472 ones and spend their extra width on columns instead: rate at 280, ETA and queue position as two mono 20/24 lines at 384, then the 48 glyph. At 472 those columns do not exist and the single meta line (288px, ~24 mono characters) carries one fact, so a download or failure clause evicts runtime and air date. The 128x72 backdrop rung stays off every row for the reason the brief gives — 96 − 2×16 = 64 < 72 — and the leading visual is the 48px disc carrying the episode number, which is also the only place ResultEpisode.episode (ResultFragment.kt:44, non-null) needs to appear, freeing the meta line of "S2·E51".

Every per-item verb on 6d is a 96x96 cell because a remote has no long-press. Today play, pause, resume, delete and cancel-pending are all items in an anchored PopupMenu built at PieFetchButton.kt:171-234 and DownloadQueueAdapter.kt:125-191, rendered through UIHelper.popupMenuNoIcons (UIHelper.kt:628-654) with no TV branch and no setDefaultFocus(); multi-select is long-press only (DownloadAdapter.kt:98-101, :349-352) and queue reorder is drag-only (DownloadQueueAdapter.kt:219-273, isLongPressDragEnabled() = true), so on a remote it is unreachable outright. 6d puts three cells per row at x1496/1616/1736 ending flush at 1832, with 24 between them; column B is the state-dependent verb (pause → resume → retry → cancel) and never moves, so the D-pad path does not change under the user's thumb. That matters most on the failed row: column B is the only re-entry into a dead download. 6c's "Select" chip and 6e's "Free up space" are the D-pad entries into DownloadViewModel.handleMultiDelete (:433, confirm dialog :547-586), which today has none.

Prerequisites these screens imply, each one a real gap. Downloaded-ness on an episode row needs downloadState on ResultEpisode (ResultFragment.kt:40-65 has no such field) plus a batch getDownloadStates(ids) in DownloadManager.kt to replace the per-button suspend read at BaseFetchButton.kt:82 — and BaseDiffCallback compares model equality (EpisodeAdapter.kt:77-81), so without the field the list can never repaint on a download change. TV must also be allowed to show it: hasDownloadSupport is hard-coded false at ResultFragmentTv.kt:433. Per-episode failure needs loadLinks to return a Resource (ResultViewModel2.kt:1265-1312) instead of swallowing the throwable at :1299-1305, and the panel's own error and retry need _episodes to receive Resource.Failure, which it never does — all three load failures post to _page only (:2630-2664), leaving the list stuck in Loading under a whole-page error. Rate and ETA exist but never leave the notification layer: bytesPerSecond is computed at DownloadManager.kt:689-692 and getEstimatedTimeLeft at DownloadUtils.kt:61-94, both consumed only by createDownloadNotification, while the UI event is a bare Triple(id, downloaded, total) (:210, :735). Queue position is the array index in DownloadQueueManager's StateFlow (:42-48) and is surfaced nowhere. A typed failure reason does not exist at all — seven assignment sites (:1198, :1253, :1383, :1426, :1462, :1751 and the bare catch at :1249-1254) collapse into one enum value, and DownloadStatus (DownloadObjects.kt:184-191) stays private to the manager — so 6b's "storage full / 1.2 GB short" and 6e's shortfall line are both blocked on it, as is the pre-flight check: nothing in utils/downloader/ reads StatFs, and failedStatus() (:558-561, DOWNLOAD_PARTIAL_MIN_SIZE at :190-191) currently records any download that dies past 50 MB as complete, so a disk-full truncation is filed as a success and the storage-full banner would never fire for the exact case it describes. Offline needs a connectivity observer, of which there is none in ui/download/ or ui/result/. Finally D-pad: nextDown = FOCUS_SELF on the TV episode list (ResultFragmentTv.kt:365-371, FOCUS_SELF resolving to the focused view itself at LinearListLayout.kt:98) swallows Down at the end of the list, so 6a's Done row at y928 is unreachable until that targets it, and TV_EP_SIZE = 400 (EpisodeAdapter.kt:66, applied :146-152 and :374-377) pins the row width against the 472 panel column.

Focus order, board by board. 6a - the panel owns focus while it is mounted, so the two behind-scrim buttons are inert: 1-50 ⇄ 51-100 on Left/Right, Down from either enters row 51, rows 51→55 on Down, and Down from 55 crosses the 56 gap to Done at y928. Every one of those boxes spans x1392..1864, so each move has full horizontal overlap. 6b is a specimen sheet - its rows are drawn, not wired, and the one ringed tile marks the focused state, not a stop in an order. 6c - Download queue ⇄ Play from URL ⇄ Select, Down into row 1, rows 1→6 on Down, and Down from row 6 scrolls the 7th up out of the peek. 6d - Back (x200..296) Down to Play next (x200..440, overlapping it) ⇄ Delete all ⇄ Retry failed (1); Down enters row 1, and inside a row the order is row ⇄ play ⇄ state verb ⇄ delete, with the column held across Down. 6e - Play from link (x1064..1464) ⇄ Queue (0/3) (x1488..1864); Down from Queue lands on Free up space (x1520..1840, wholly inside it) and Down from Play from link lands on Browse library (x872..1192, overlapping at x1064..1192). Both state actions therefore have a real overlapping neighbour above; before this pass the chrome band started at x1256 and the centred empty-state button had none.

States, where they are not drawn. 6a: loading is the same five slots at 296/416/536/656/776 as line skeleton bars on the real anatomy, so nothing reflows on arrival — TV has no episode loading state today, ResultFragmentTv.kt:811 is commented out. Offline and partial ride row slot 1 as a 96-tall banner, which costs exactly one 120 pitch and drops the list to four rows at 416/536/656/776; the copy is range-scoped ("Offline — 9 of 50 downloaded") because getEpisodes only ever returns the selected range's sublist (:1877-1889). Empty and error take a panel-scoped headline at Manrope 40/56 (not the chassis 64/88, which wraps to four lines in 472) at y296 h112, body 24/32 at y416 h64, and their action in row slot 2 at y536 — a slot already in the focus order — because the season and range chips are both conditionally hidden (:2036-2039, :2045-2053) and on a one-season show under 60 episodes neither exists. 6c: loading is six skeleton rows at the row geometry with the storage meter running its own; empty and error share one state view centred on x1032 at y496/608/728 (6e, which carries a 96 glyph, runs 440/568/680/800). 6e draws empty, offline and error (storage-full) together; loading is the same 96-tall band with an undivided line bar and the legends suppressed, so the state view below never moves, and partial is genuinely N/A on a screen with zero items. 6b is a component board and owes no surface states; the row skeleton is drawn on 6a rather than here — a ninth specimen would push the 1664 failed row past y1024, and that is a decision, not a definition.

And one defect no geometry scanner could have found. This file measured 0 off-grid, 0 clipped and 0 band violations while --mut was #686868 — 3.77:1 on bg and 3.17:1 on the raised fill, against the 4.5:1 AA floor for normal-size text. It carries every episode's runtime and air date, every queue position, the storage legend and both chip counts: the entire second line of this cluster. It is now #858585 — 5.69 / 5.18 / 4.78. A colour moves no boxes, which is exactly why it survived every measurement pass. See §08 of the design system for the audit of all nine tokens.
