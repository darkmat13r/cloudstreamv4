Search on the phone. The field is a pill at the top, focused when the tab opens, with the type chips scrolling under it. Recent searches fill the page before typing (PS1). Results are a three-column grid grouped by source (PS2): two would show too little, and four too small to read. Sources is a chip that opens a bottom sheet with a checkbox per source (PS3). Each row's target is the whole row, at 64 tall.

Library on the phone. The source is a chip with its sync line under it; Filter and Sort are 48 icon cells. The lists are chips that scroll. Cards are three to a line, with the watched rule (PL1). Sort is a bottom sheet (PL2). A failed sync is a banner with Retry sync, with the cards kept and dimmed (PL3).

The tablet uses the navigation rail and the Mac's order at touch sizes: six columns at 1280 (TS1, TL1).

Measured in the task entry: boxes on the 4 dp step except the bottom navigation's derived cells, as DS-18 recorded.
