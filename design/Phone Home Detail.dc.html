Home on the phone. The source is a chip at the top that opens a bottom sheet (P2), with None first. Reload and Random are 48 icon cells beside it. Rows are horizontal rails, because a thumb swipes sideways; the hero is a card with Play and More info. Offline is a pill, and a failed reload is a banner with Try again, the rows kept (P3).

Detail on the phone is the direction ui-components.md already wrote down for it: a full-width Resume with the time left under it, then Start over, then a row of glyphs with their labels underneath (Watchlist, Trailer, Download, Share, More), then the synopsis and the episodes. The season is a chip that opens a sheet; each episode row has its one verb as a 48 cell. Sources are a bottom sheet that grows as links arrive (PD2).

The tablet at 840 dp and wider uses the navigation rail. Home keeps the Mac's order at touch sizes; a title's page puts the art and actions in a left column and the episodes beside it, with Play and Download as 48 cells on each row (rule 4's list-row exception, extended to the tablet by the owner, DEC-19).

Measured in the task entry: boxes on the 4 dp step except widths derived from the screen (the glyph row's five equal cells), as DS-18 recorded for its bottom navigation.
