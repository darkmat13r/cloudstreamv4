DS-18Phone and tablet — Downloads

Phone and tablet boards for downloads, at 1 px = 1 dp. The phone uses touch: bottom navigation, rows you tap, swipe and long-press, and sheets from the bottom edge. The window's width class decides: below 600 is the phone (P1–P6), 600 to 840 is the phone layout with wider margins, and 840 or more is the two-pane tablet (T1). Same tokens, the mobile type column. Glyphs come from the Tabler pack.

What the phone does that the Mac does not. Swipe to delete, long-press to choose, bottom sheets for verbs, and bottom navigation. The touch target is the visual, 48 dp, so there is no focus ring and no hover. Feedback is a ripple on press.

What the three share, in code. DownloadsViewModel, DownloadSeriesViewModel, their contracts and DownloadShelf as they are. A new commonMain mapper puts each row's words in one place for all three: the state, "4.24 GB of 5.10 GB" and "Series · 12 episodes". The tokens, ProgressRule, StatusPill, StorageMeter, StateView, FailureBanner, the card art and CloudStreamIcons are shared too. Each device has its own row: the Mac's table row with hover verbs, the phone's swipe row, and TV's 96 focus row as it is. A shared row would need flags for all three, and DEC-19 rules that out.

Measured, and the exemptions. All boxes are on the 4 dp step except two families, both derived from the screen's width and not typed. The four bottom-navigation cells are weight(1f) each, so they are 103 at 412. The two secondaries in P4 split the row equally, 186 each. Neither is a number anyone writes, which is the grid's own exemption for intrinsic widths. The tablet's trailing glyphs (T1) are icon-only list-row actions, the ruled exception in ui-components.md rule 4. They are applied off TV here, so that needs the owner's yes.

Not drawn, and owed by the same rows. Loading is a skeleton of the rows on all three. A failed read is StateView with Try again in the list's place. Partial is not possible here, because there is one source.
