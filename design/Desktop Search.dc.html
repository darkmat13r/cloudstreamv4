From TV Search (4a to 4d) and its suggestions (8a to 8c), for a window. The field is in the window's bar and holds focus from the start, as the built SearchScreenDesktop does: typing is the point. Type chips sit under the bar. Results are a grid, not rails, grouped by source with each group's count, because a mouse wheel scrolls one way. Titles stay visible, since there is no D-pad focus to reveal them.

Sources becomes a button (S4) that opens the side panel the app uses for a choice from a list. The built screen shows a read-out instead, because it has no picker yet. A failed source keeps its row, with the reason in words and a hollow glyph.

Suggestions (S5) are a popover under the field: one ranked column, recent and suggested together, the arrow keys moving through it and Return searching what is typed. Its status line counts what answered, so the counts on screen add up.

Failure stays legible. A partial search (S3) is a banner that names what failed and retries exactly that. No results (S6) says every source answered, and names the filter that narrowed it.

Not drawn here: Quick Search (8d, 8e). On TV it is a panel over another screen; on a Mac, ⌘F from any screen brings the window to Search with the field focused, which these boards already show.
