From TV Library (5a to 5e), for a window. The bar holds the library's source (AniList, Local or another tracker), when it last synced, a filter field and Sort. The lists are a row of buttons that stays through every state, as the built LibraryScreenDesktop has them: changing list is the way out of a list that failed or is empty.

Cards are a grid, as many to a line as the window allows (the built minimum is 200). Each shows its watched rule in live and how far along it is. Hovering lifts the card 4 and shows its one button, Remove, which removes it from the list you are looking at. A click resumes on Continue watching and opens the title's page anywhere else, as built.

Sort, the source, and moving a card (L4, L5, L6) are side panels, as every desktop choice from a list is. A criterion the source does not store stays in the list, greyed, with the reason, rather than disappearing.

Failure stays legible. A sync that failed (L2) keeps the cards, dims them so nothing pretends to be live, and says how old they are. An empty list (L3) says what lands there and offers the list that has something.
