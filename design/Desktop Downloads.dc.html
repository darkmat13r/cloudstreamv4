DS-18Desktop — Downloads

Desktop boards for downloads, drawn at 1 px = 1 dp (macOS points), not the TV's px ÷ 2. The colours, the 4 dp step and the desktop type column are the design system's. Layout follows the owner's direction (2026-11-02): Apple's principles, so a pointer, a keyboard and a resizable window drive it. These boards do not mirror TV's 6c–6e; they say the same facts in a Mac's way. Glyphs are the Tabler pack itself, loaded at 1.119.0, never drawn.

D1 · Downloads, titles, a row hovered1280×800, the window's default. Downloads is the fourth destination, so the bar becomes a sidebar (desktop-design.md: "a sidebar is worth it at the fourth"). One 56 row per title, columns for name, status and size, so a scan down the window answers what is still moving. The row's verbs appear under the pointer, never moving the text: resume or retry, Show in Finder, Delete. Double-click or Return opens a title.

What the Mac does that TV and phone do not. Hover reveals verbs. Selection is the pointer's own (click, ⌘, ⇧, ⌘A), with no Select chip. Right-click gives a context menu that acts on the selection. Keys: Return opens, Space plays a finished title, ⌘⌫ deletes (it asks first), ⌘R shows in Finder, ⌘[ goes back, ⌘4 opens Downloads. The menu bar lists the same commands under File, so every one is discoverable without hovering.

Why Cancel is the default in D3. The delete removes files from the disk and cannot be undone. Apple's guidance is that the default button in a destructive confirmation is the safe one. Here that is also the one filled button, so rule 1 still holds.

Departures from 6c–6e, each deliberate. No queue chip row: Queue is a toolbar button, and the count sits in it. No 96×96 verb cells on every series row: on a pointer they are noise until the pointer arrives. No "Select" chip, for the reason above.

Open for the owner. (1) Folder is a proposed CloudStreamIcons entry, Tabler Folder, for Show in Finder; nothing in the table means it today. (2) New strings, all through :core:strings: Show in Finder, Delete N downloads?, This frees %s and can't be undone, N of M selected, Open Storage Settings, Nothing downloaded yet, and the empty line. (3) A row's hover verbs are glyph-only 24 cells, which is DesktopDimens.FieldActionSize, holding a 16 glyph. A 36 DesktopIconButton cannot centre on the step in a 56 or a 48 row. Each verb carries its name as a tooltip and a content description, and the menu bar and context menu name it in words. That extends ui-components.md rule 4's ruled exception for trailing list-row actions to the desktop. (4) The transfer rate in D4 needs KMP-52 to report it. DownloadTitle has no rate today, so the titles list does not show one.
